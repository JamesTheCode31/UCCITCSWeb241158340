Welcome to Our Product!

  • Ilocos empanada is a famous Filipino street food from the Ilocos Region.
  • It has a bright orange crispy rice flour shell.
  • Filled with grated green papaya, mung beans, longganisa sausage, and a whole egg.
  • Deep-fried to a crisp and served with spiced Ilocos vinegar (sukang Iloko).
